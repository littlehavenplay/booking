The desert parent problem

Out here the weather decides your day. It’s 106 by ten in the morning, or it’s blowing sand sideways, and either way the kids have exactly the same amount of energy.

So you drive to the park and turn around. You try the living room and last forty minutes. There’s nowhere built for children this small that you actually want to sit in.
