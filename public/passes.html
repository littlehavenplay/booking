Loyalty Punch Card

No cards to buy — just play! Pay your normal admission each visit, and your 8th visit is on us. 🎈

How it works

  1. Pay your normal admission each visit — nothing to buy upfront.
  2. Give your child's name when you book or check in.
  3. We add a punch for each paid visit. After 7 visits, your 8th visit is free!
  4. When you earn it, we email you a free-visit code — enter it at checkout on your next online booking and one child's admission is on us.
