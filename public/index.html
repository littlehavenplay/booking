FAQ

Helpful things to know before you visit

Anything else? Email hello@littlehavenplay.com

🗓️ Booking, Hours & Visits
What are your hours?

Hours can change for events and seasons — current hours are always at the top of this page. Last admission is 1 hour before closing.

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 1 week in advance.

Do I need to book online?

Booking online is recommended. Walk-ins are welcome when there’s space.

How long is a visit?

Up to 2 hours, starting when you arrive. Running a few minutes late is fine.

What if I don’t show up?

If you haven’t checked in within 1 hour of your arrival time, your spot is released. See our policies.

🎟️ Admission & Pricing
What ages can play?

Ages 0–6 or up to 48" tall. Older kids are welcome in the lobby but can’t play in the play area.

Do babies under 6 months need admission?

No — babies 0–5 months come free.

How many adults can come?

Each Regular or Baby admission includes 2 adults. Extra adults can be added at checkout.

What is the Sibling Add-On?

A discounted admission for a brother or sister on the same visit. It must be booked with a Regular admission.

What payment do you accept?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, and all major credit & debit cards.

How does the loyalty punch card work?

It’s free and automatic. After 7 visits, your child’s 8th visit is free — we’ll email you the code.

Do you offer a birthday gift?

Yes! Add your child’s birthday when you book and we’ll email a free admission code for their birthday week.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes, 10% off. Show your military ID at the desk once and it applies to future online bookings.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Share your code from littlehavenplay.com/refer — new families get $5 off their first visit, and you get $5 in credit when they come play.

Are event tickets refundable?

Event tickets are non-refundable. Can’t make it? Email us and we’ll do our best to help.

💚 Play Club Membership
What is the Play Club?

A monthly membership for unlimited open play. See plans at littlehavenplay.com/playclub.

How do I book as a member?

Enter your membership phone number or code in the Play Club box when you book, then tick who’s coming.

Weekday vs. Any Day plan?

Weekday covers Monday–Friday. Any Day covers every day we’re open.

Can someone else bring my child?

Yes. They enter their own name and contact info, and use your membership phone number in the Play Club box.

Can I bring an extra child?

Yes — just add them as a regular admission when you book.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes! Each child on your membership gets one free buddy pass a month for a friend on the same visit.

Do members get a party discount?

Yes, 10% off a private party. It comes off your final balance, not the deposit.

I just joined — why can’t I book yet?

Please wait for your welcome email. Your membership is ready to use once it arrives.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from your Square receipt or email us. You can keep playing through the month you’ve paid for.

🛡️ Safety & Studio Rules
Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes, before playing. Sign it online here to save time.

Are grip socks required?

Yes, for all walking children. The grips must cover the bottom of the foot. Grip socks are sold at the studio and online, and adults must wear socks in the play area.

Is this a drop-off facility?

No. A parent or guardian 18+ must stay and supervise. The only exception is our special drop-off events.

Can I bring a stroller?

Space is limited, so please leave large strollers in the car. Compact folding strollers are fine.

Is there a changing station?

Yes, in our restroom.

Can sick children come?

Please stay home if your child has a fever, vomiting, a rash, or other contagious symptoms.

Can adults play on the equipment?

No, the equipment is for little ones — unless your child needs help.

🎉 Private Parties
How far ahead do I book a party?

At least 2 weeks. Your date is held once the deposit is paid.

Can packages be changed?

Packages are as listed, but add-ons are available. See party packages.

Is open play available during a party?

No — the studio is private during parties.
