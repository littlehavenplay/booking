Party FAQ

How far in advance do I book, and how is my date held?

Parties must be booked at least 2 weeks in advance. A deposit reserves your date and time — your party isn't confirmed until the deposit is paid.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are non-refundable unless you cancel at least 2 weeks before the party date. Cancellations made less than 2 weeks before the party are not eligible for a deposit refund. The deposit applies toward your package total.

When is the rest of the balance due?

The remaining balance is due on the day of the party. Any approved add-ons are charged at the end of the event.

Can I customize or change a package?

Party packages cannot be substituted, modified, or customized. Add-ons may be available and can be added to an existing package.

What if I'm running late?

Party time begins and ends at the scheduled reserved time; late arrival doesn't extend it. Extra time may be available only if approved in advance and may carry an additional fee.

What if I need to cancel for an emergency?

For illness, emergencies, or unexpected circumstances, Little Haven may offer store credit at our discretion, usable toward a future booking, Open Play, punch card, or other eligible purchase.

What if Little Haven has to cancel?

If we must cancel due to an emergency, facility issue, or unexpected closure, you may reschedule, receive store credit, or a refund of any amount paid.
